Privacy policy

Effective November 8, 2026. Applies to fineprinteagle.com and the Fine Print Eagle newsletter.

This is short on purpose. We read other people’s fine print for a living, so ours should be readable too.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you ask for the Fine Print Checklist or join the newsletter. We also note which platform you came from (for example YouTube or Instagram) so we know where our readers find us.
  • Your first name, only if a form asks for it and you type it in. It is optional.
  • A cookie-free count of visits. When you open a page or tap one of our buttons, the page sends a small note to our own counting service: which page, which button, which platform sent you (from the link you used or the site you came from), whether you are on a phone or a computer, your country, and the time. To tell one visitor from another for a single day, we store a scrambled code made from your internet address and browser type. The code changes every day and cannot be turned back into your address. We never store your internet address itself.
  • Email activity our email provider records automatically: that a message was delivered, opened, or a link in it was clicked, plus the date and the IP address when you confirmed your subscription. We keep that confirmation record as proof that you asked to be on the list.

There are no advertising pixels, no Google Analytics, no Meta or TikTok trackers and no tracking cookies on this website. The counting is first-party: the numbers go only to us, on a service we run, and we use them for one thing, seeing which platforms and buttons work. The site’s pages are hosted on Render, which, like any web host, keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and operations.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the Fine Print Checklist after you confirm your address (double opt-in).
  • To send the weekly Fine Print Eagle email: one money rule, the numbers behind it, one thing to do. Some issues include a clearly separated, labeled promotion for a sponsor or for the writer’s own business. You can skip those; the rest is education.
  • To see which topics people read and which platforms bring readers here, so we spend our time on the things that matter.

Who handles it

Signups and emails run through Kit (Kit.com, formerly ConvertKit), our email service provider. Kit stores your address on our behalf and processes it under its own privacy policy. Our signup form sends your address to Kit, and at the same time we keep our own record of the address, the date and the platform you came from on Cloudflare, the service that runs our visit counting. That record exists so we can see which platform each signup came from. Cloudflare processes it under its privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your email address or name.
  • We never share your address with sponsors. A sponsor sees how many people clicked, not who.
  • We never send text messages or make calls. We do not ask for your phone number.
  • We never store your internet address alongside your visits, and we never follow you across other websites.

How to unsubscribe or delete your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link in the footer. One click, no login, no questions. If you want your record deleted entirely rather than just unsubscribed, email eagle@fineprinteagle.com from the address in question and we will remove it from Kit and from our own signup record within 10 business days.

Cookies and embedded videos

This website sets no cookies. Our visit counting uses none either; the only thing the page remembers, until you close the tab, is which platform sent you, so a signup can be credited to it. The home page embeds a YouTube video using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube and Google may still set cookies or collect data once you press play, under Google’s privacy policy. If you would rather not, watch on YouTube directly or skip the video.

Links to other sites

We link to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Kit and, in the newsletter, to government and news sources. Their policies apply on their pages, not ours.

Children

Fine Print Eagle is for adults managing their own money. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has subscribed, email us and we will remove the address.

California and “Do Not Track”

If you live in California, you may ask what personal information we hold about you and request its deletion; the answer will be your email address, possibly a first name, the platform you signed up from, and the email activity described above. Email eagle@fineprinteagle.com. Our site does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals because the visit count is anonymous and we do not track you across other sites in the first place.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new effective date. If the change affects how we use your address, we will say so in the newsletter.
